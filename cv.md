## Work

**Staff writer**, *Correspondent Magazine* — 2021–present
Long-form features on politics, migration, and culture for a European affairs monthly. Print and digital editions. Average piece length 4,000–8,000 words.

**Contributing editor**, *Fieldwork Journal* — 2019–2021
Commission and edit pieces from the journal's network of regional correspondents. Responsible for the Caucasus and Central Asia desk.

**Freelance reporter** — 2015–2019
Reported from Georgia, Armenia, Moldova, Ukraine, and Serbia. Published in *Die Zeit*, *Politiken*, *The Guardian* (long reads), and others.
